## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro, static output. The site is mostly content (services, portfolio, about, team, contact), and Astro ships plain HTML/CSS with no runtime by default. It gives components and content collections for portfolio pieces and services, which the client will keep adding, without a heavy framework, and deploys to any static host. No deploy target confirmed yet. It replaces an existing WordPress site at https://oqueeupostariamkt.com.br/.

## Users

Business owners and professionals across Brazil who want to build their digital positioning: people who value branding and either share knowledge (lawyers, consultants, engineers, photographers, experts) or create experiences through their products/services. They arrive looking for a partner to make their communication strategic, and should leave convinced enough to contact the team by WhatsApp or email for a proposal.

This site is being built for a client (the agency), not by the agency itself.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for **O Que Eu Postaria? — Agência de Comunicação e Marketing** (short form: OQEP). Its mission, in the agency's words: "simplificar a comunicação para empresas e profissionais que buscam se destacar nas redes sociais," offering effective strategies to strengthen online presence and sharing technical knowledge to reach results.

Success: qualified businesses/professionals contact the team to request a proposal ("Prepararemos uma proposta especial para você").

## Positioning

- Strategy comes first: "elaborar a estratégia é um serviço prévio ao serviço de gestão de redes." A 20–30 day strategy phase (channels, personality, competitors, objectives, personas, SWOT, brand DNA, keywords, visual identity, editorial lines, tone of voice, channel strategy, site mapping, funnel campaigns) precedes execution. Solutions "vão além da produção de conteúdo."
- Journalism roots: founded by a journalist, the agency also does press relations (assessoria de imprensa), journalistic writing and text revision, which most social-media agencies cannot claim.
- Origin story as proof: the founder's Instagram @oqueeupostaria.mkt started as free content tips with no commercial goal and grew fast enough to become the agency.
- Humanized, dedicated service; honesty and transparency; personalized action plans; strategy, organization, planning and strict deadlines.
- Success is measured by **length of partnership and client trust**: long-term strategic partner, not a one-off vendor.

## Operating Context

- Founded 08 February 2021 in Porto Alegre/RS by journalist **Andréia Ramires**. Serves clients nationwide (remote). Site language: Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR).
- Sales flow: visitor contacts a named person by WhatsApp or email (or a contact form), and the agency prepares a custom proposal. No public pricing.

## Product Principles

1. Strategy before posts: show thinking and process, not just output.
2. Partnership over transaction: long-term trust is the proof point.
3. Human and transparent: real names, real people to talk to, plain language, no hype.
4. Each client is particular: emphasize personalization over packaged formulas.
5. Built to grow: services and portfolio pieces are added over time without redesign.
