## Direction contract

THESIS: The agency as a sticker album you complete. Team, clients and work are figurinhas with numbers, names and positions; the home is the album's opening spread. Refuses the dark-ground agency page with a case grid and logo marquee.

OWN-WORLD: Album pages printed in terracotta #AE361B with deep-terracotta print shadows, white-bordered stickers with rounded corners and a slight gloss, numbered empty slots with silhouette outlines, yellow foil for the one "brilhante" sticker (Andréia), ink-black name bars in condensed caps, extended heavy caps for page titles (the portfolio PDF's voice), checklist pages for dense lists.

STORY: The visitor sees the question that names the agency answered by a team that plans before it posts, opens a pack and sees real work stick into the album, scans every service as the album checklist, meets the team, reads two clients in their own words, then subscribes or messages a real person.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Terracotta album cover. Top: logo crest left, album nav, WhatsApp right. Left two-thirds: "O QUE EU POSTARIA?" at maximum display size, one-line answer below, newsletter field + button inline, WhatsApp text link under it. Right: a sealed sticker pack (logo printed on foil) leaning at an angle with three real work stickers fanned behind it. Clicking or dragging tears the pack and five stickers spring out. Next section's album page edge visible at the fold.

FORM: Álbum de Figurinhas, ranked 1 on my ordered list (presented as the pick, chosen over the re-rolled assignment). Seed key 11993505. Signature interaction: tearing the pack, stickers fly with spring physics and each can be "colada" into its numbered slot in the portfolio album. Motion grammar: spring drop with overshoot (rotate from -10°, scale 1.3 → 1), only on stickers.
